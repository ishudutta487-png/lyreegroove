ABOUT

The story behind the music, and why this exists.

The Band

  • Current Members
    • Mike Shinoda – vocals, keyboards, rhythm guitar, production
    • Rob Bourdon – drums, percussion
    • Brad Delson – lead guitar, backing vocals
    • Joe Hahn – turntables, samples, programming
    • Dave "Phoenix" Farrell – bass guitar, backing vocals
    • Emily Armstrong – co-lead vocals, keyboards
    • Colin Brittain – drums, percussion


  • Former Members
    • Chester Bennington: Lead vocals (1999–2017; passed away in 2017)
    • Rob Bourdon: Drums (1996–2017; departed after Bennington's death)
    • Mark Wakefield: Lead vocals (1996–1998)
    • Kyle Christner: Bass (1999)

    • Linkin Park's journey began in 1996 in Agoura Hills, California, when high school friends Mike Shinoda, Rob Bourdon, and Brad Delson formed a band called Xero, later joined by Joe Hahn, Dave "Phoenix" Farrell, and original lead vocalist Wakefield. After Wakefield's departure, Arizona vocalist Chester Bennington joined in 1999, prompting a name change to Hybrid Theory and eventually Linkin Park.

    Their sound evolved dramatically across eight studio albums, transitioning from the aggressive, rap-rock and hip-hop fusions of Hybrid Theory (2000) and Meteora (2003) into a softer, alternative rock approach on Minutes to Midnight (2007). They pivoted to deeply experimental, electronic concept landscapes on A Thousand Suns (2010) and Living Things (2012), pivoted backward into raw, aggressive punk-metal riffs on The Hunting Party (2014), and briefly embraced radio-friendly pop textures on One More Light (2017). Following a seven-year hiatus after Bennington's passing, the band entered a new era with their 2024 album From Zero, introducing co-lead vocalist Emily Armstrong and drummer Colin Brittain to blend their classic energetic nu-metal roots with modern rock dynamics.

    The band's distinctive songwriting process has always relied heavily on a democratic, collaborative chemistry, driven by Mike Shinoda's meticulous production alongside the distinct vocal interplay between rap verses and soaring, emotional choruses. This meticulous studio execution translates seamlessly into their legendary live performances, which are renowned for their raw energy, heavy crowd interaction, and flawless technical delivery. From the early days of headlining the Projekt Revolution tour to their massive 2024–2026 From Zero World Tour stadiums, their live shows have functioned as powerful sonic sanctuaries, binding generations of fans together through a shared catharsis of screaming lyrics that tackle mental health, vulnerability, and resilience.
